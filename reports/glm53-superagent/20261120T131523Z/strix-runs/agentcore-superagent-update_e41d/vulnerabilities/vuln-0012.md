## Description

`nodemailer` 8.0.1 is pinned in `super-agent/backend/package-lock.json` and covered by advisory CVE-2026-82662 (fixed in 8.0.8). The backend sends transactional emails with it.

## Evidence

**Advisory evidence:** `CVE-2026-82662` applies to `nodemailer` at installed version `8.0.1`. The advisory is fixed in `8.0.8`.

**Usage analysis:** the advisory's vulnerable code runs in this codebase. super-agent/backend/src/services/email.service.ts:8 import; createTransport at email.service.ts:20; sendMail at email.service.ts:67, invoked by application flows (invite emails) with user-influenced recipient addresses. Gated by SMTP being configured (email.service.ts:15).

## Impact

The vulnerable nodemailer code runs whenever SMTP is configured and the backend sends invite/notification emails; exploitation is confined to the email sending path.

## Contextual CVSS

nodemailer is imported once, in super-agent/backend/src/services/email.service.ts:8, and used through `nodemailer.createTransport` at email.service.ts:20 and `sendMail` at email.service.ts:67. The mail path fires from application flows (e.g. org invite emails) whose recipient address is user-influenced, but SMTP must be configured (`config.smtp.enabled` gate at email.service.ts:15) and the advisory's vulnerable path requires the specific flaw conditions, so attack complexity is raised to H and impact limited to the email subsystem.

## Remediation

Bump nodemailer to at least 8.0.8 (or the 9.x/10.x line per the other advisories) in super-agent/backend and regenerate the lockfile.
